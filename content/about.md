If you somehow got to this site and don't know me, hi, I'm Angelin01!

## Who are you?

I enjoy computer stuff, some hardware stuff (but know way too little about it), game stuff, TTRPG stuff, and dragon stuff, the last one mostly because of Toothless.

I try to keep my personal stuff private, to some extent, so that's all the details you'll get! On the technical side, I wear all the DevOps hats: SREs, Platform Engineers, etc. All the same set of skills if you ask me, you just change your focus and main goals, but the work is similar. On the personal side, I'm usually the one setting up stuff: organizing game servers, running TTRPG games, calling people to play co-op. It's mostly out of necessity, not desire, but it is what it is.

## Why does this website look like THIS?

The entire style of this website is a homage to [Dwarf Fortress](https://www.bay12games.com/dwarves/). It doesn't match the style 1:1, especially not the new versions or the Steam release, but I think it carries the spirit.

Dwarf Fortress is not my most played game, not by a longshot, but it is definitely my favorite. I go on and on about its systems and what it does, but that would bore you, so instead let me just summarize that I think it deserves a spot as one of the best games of all time, for damn good reasons.

The style is also sort of inspired by [Tramoia](https://tramoia.sh/), a Brazilian blog about crypto security. I'm gonna be honest, the content of the website goes right over my head, but I think it looks beautiful. Check it out! I played with the fluid simulations a little too much.

## But why does the website even exist?

I have a tendency to rant. A lot. My friends have pointed this out. My coworkers have pointed this out. It has become kind of an internal joke in various separate friend groups, actually. So, instead of ranting to many people separately, here I can rant to all of them, all at once! It scales _so much better_, don't you think?

The topics are going to be varied, probably whatever I feel like writing at the time. If I'm putting together a Minecraft modpack, it's going to be about that. If I'm arguing with somebody about some techy thing, probably that. If I just learned about something new, maybe that too! So consistency _will not_ be a focus of the posts here.

Oh, by the way, here be rants and here be easter eggs. Click around, audio on! Have fun!
